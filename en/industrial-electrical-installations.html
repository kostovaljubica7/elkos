Electrical installations for industrial projects

Industrial facilities differ in their purpose, size and project requirements. ELKOS provides design and installation of electrical systems, with an approach considered for the property and the client’s needs.

To discuss a project, it helps to understand the facility type, current stage and any available project documentation. This information provides a starting point for discussing planning, materials and the installation scope.
