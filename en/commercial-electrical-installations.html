Solutions for work and customer-facing spaces

Every commercial space has its own purpose and requirements. ELKOS provides electrical installation design and installation for offices, shops and other business properties. The work is discussed based on the premises, available plans and the client’s needs.

For a new business property or changes to an existing space, the electrical installation scope should be considered alongside the project for the premises. Information about the type of business and the project stage helps identify the next steps and materials to discuss.

Offices

Planning and installation for workspaces based on the project and the client’s needs.

Shops

Considering electrical installations in relation to the layout and purpose of the business space.

Other business premises

An approach based on the specific property, its purpose and the planned work.

What to prepare

  • The type and purpose of the business premises.
  • The location and whether the space is being built, fitted out or renovated.
  • Available plans and a short description of the electrical installation needs.
