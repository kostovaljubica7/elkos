Electrical Installation Design

ELKOS provides detailed electrical installation planning and design based on the property and the client’s individual requirements.

Planning around the property and your needs

Good planning starts with understanding the intended use of the property and the client’s requirements. ELKOS designs electrical installations for residential, commercial and industrial properties, with materials considered as part of the planned solution.

For an initial discussion, share available plans, the property type and your requirements. This information helps establish the design scope and possible next steps.

Client requirements

Planning takes account of individual requirements and how the space will be used.

Property characteristics

The design is considered in the context of the type and layout of the specific property.

Materials

Materials are considered as part of the planned electrical installation solution.

What to share for an initial discussion

  • The type and purpose of the property.
  • Available architectural or other project plans.
  • Any specific electrical installation requirements.
