Electrical installations planned for your home

An electrical installation should suit the layout, plans and intended use of a home. ELKOS works on the design and installation of electrical systems for residential properties, with the solution considered for each individual project.

Whether you are planning a new house or apartment, or changes to an existing property, the first step is to discuss the building and the work involved. Available plans and information about your needs help guide a conversation about the project, materials and installation.

Design

Planning the electrical installation around the property and the client’s requirements.

Installation

Professional installation of electrical systems as part of your home project.

Materials

Discussing the electrical materials needed for the planned solution.

How to request a quote

  1. Tell us whether the property is a house or apartment and its current stage.
  2. Describe what you need and share a plan or scope of work if available.
  3. Contact ELKOS to discuss the project scope and next steps.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on new and existing homes?

ELKOS provides design and installation for residential properties. Contact us with a description of your property to discuss the specific scope.

What should I share for an initial discussion?

The property type and location, whether it is under construction or being renovated, and any plans or a short description of the work are helpful.
